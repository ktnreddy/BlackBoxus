Built so you don't have to trust us

Most password managers ask you to trust their servers. BlackBoxus is designed to stay safe even if ours is broken into or misbehaves.

Only you hold the keys

Your vaults are opened by two secrets: your master password and a Secret Key made on your first device. Neither is ever sent to us, so a stolen copy of our database is just noise.

Catches a lying server

Every change to a vault is signed and chained to the one before. Your apps replay the whole history, so items can't be swapped, hidden or rolled back without you noticing.

Share with people you've checked

Everyone's public keys are recorded in a public, append-only log. Before you share a vault you compare a short safety number, so nobody can slip in their own key.

A plan for the worst

Print an Emergency Kit, keep an optional recovery key, and name a trusted contact who can ask for access after a waiting period that you can cancel.

Ready for what's next

Encryption keys combine today's proven algorithms with new post-quantum ones, so recordings of your data stay useless even to future quantum computers.

Open for anyone to check

The apps, the server and the full protocol specification are free software under the GNU AGPL, and every parser of untrusted data is fuzz-tested.

Everything you expect, done carefully

The everyday parts of a password manager, built so secrets stay where they belong.

  1. Fill logins in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox ask the app for one login at a time, only for the site you're on.
  2. Unlock with Windows Hello. Face, fingerprint or PIN, with the key kept by Windows.
  3. One-time codes built in. Two-factor codes right next to the password, computed without the secret ever reaching the screen.
  4. Bring your passwords along. Import from Bitwarden, 1Password, Chrome, Firefox, LastPass, KeePass and more.
  5. Works offline. Your vaults open from your computer's encrypted copy when there's no connection.
  6. Strong passwords on tap. Random passwords or passphrases from the EFF word list.

Free, with no catch

BlackBoxus is a personal project with no ads, no tracking and no paid tier. Your vaults are yours: everything in them is encrypted, and the code that does it is open for you to read.
