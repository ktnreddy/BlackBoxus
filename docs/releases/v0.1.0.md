The first test release of BlackBoxus, for a small group of testers.

> **Please read first.** BlackBoxus hasn't been independently audited yet. Use test accounts and passwords you can afford to lose, and keep your current password manager while you try it.

## What to download

| You have | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `BlackBoxus-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Android 9 or later | `BlackBoxus-android-arm64.apk` |
| Debian, Ubuntu or Mint | `BlackBoxus-linux-amd64.deb` |
| Other Linux | `BlackBoxus-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Chrome, Edge or Brave | `BlackBoxus-extension-chrome.zip` |
| Firefox 128 or later | `BlackBoxus-extension-firefox.zip` |

Check each file against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before installing (see "Check your download" on the [download page](https://blackboxus.pages.dev/download#verify)).

## Installing

- **Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it's unrecognised. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for your account only.
- **Android:** open the APK on your phone and allow your browser to install apps when asked. Later test releases install over this one and keep your data.
- **Browser extension:** unzip it. In Chrome, Edge or Brave, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder. In Firefox, open `about:debugging`, choose **This Firefox › Load Temporary Add-on** and pick `manifest.json` (Firefox removes it when it restarts). Then turn on **Browser extension** in the app's Settings and pair it.

## What to try

1. Create an account and **save the Emergency Kit**. You need its Secret Key, with your master password, to sign in on another device.
2. Add a few logins, cards and notes, or import a CSV export from another password manager.
3. Sign in on a second device (Windows or Android) and watch changes sync.
4. Fill a login on a website with the browser extension, and in an Android app with autofill (**Settings › Autofill** in the app).
5. Share a vault with another tester, after comparing your safety numbers.
6. Turn on Windows Hello or fingerprint unlock, change your master password, make a recovery key.
7. Edit something while offline, then come back online.

## What's in it

- End-to-end encrypted vaults: the server only ever holds locked data, and the apps check every answer it gives.
- Logins, cards, notes and one-time codes, with folders, tags and custom fields.
- Sharing vaults with people whose keys you've checked; trusted emergency contacts.
- Password health: weak, reused and leaked passwords, and sites that offer two-step sign-in.
- Windows app, Android app (with autofill and QR scanning for one-time codes), Linux packages and the browser extension.

## Known limits

- Mac, iPhone and iPad apps aren't in this release.
- The browser extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons yet.
- Autofill on Android needs BlackBoxus to be unlocked first.

## Found a problem?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ktnreddy/BlackBoxus/issues/new/choose), and never paste a password, your Secret Key or anything from your Emergency Kit. If it could put someone's passwords at risk, use **Report a security problem privately** instead.
