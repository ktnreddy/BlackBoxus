Autofill on Android that actually fills, unlocking without losing your place, and Sync now on phones.

> **Please read first.** BlackBoxus hasn't been independently audited yet. Use test accounts and passwords you can afford to lose, and keep your current password manager while you try it.

## What to download

| You have | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `BlackBoxus-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Android 9 or later | `BlackBoxus-android-arm64.apk` |
| Debian, Ubuntu or Mint | `BlackBoxus-linux-amd64.deb` |
| Other Linux | `BlackBoxus-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Chrome, Edge or Brave | `BlackBoxus-extension-chrome.zip` |
| Firefox 128 or later | `BlackBoxus-extension-firefox.zip` |

Check each file against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before installing (see "Check your download" on the [download page](https://blackboxus.pages.dev/download#verify)). The Android app installs over 0.2.0 and keeps your data.

## Fixed

- **Choosing a login in "Fill with BlackBoxus" didn't fill anything.** It now fills straight away.
- **A locked BlackBoxus sent you off to unlock it, and you had to come back and tap the field again.** "Unlock BlackBoxus" now opens the app and brings you straight back once you've unlocked, then fills or saves by itself.
- **The desktop app couldn't open an encrypted export to import it.** Its file picker only showed CSV files; it now shows BlackBoxus exports (`.bbx`) too.
- **Slow on every request.** The server's database moved from North America to Singapore, so requests from India no longer cross the Pacific. This needed no update; it's already live.

## New

- **Your logins, right under the field (Android).** In a browser, the logins saved for the site are listed under the username or password field, and in the keyboard's suggestions; tap one to fill it. **Fill with BlackBoxus** opens the full list.
- **Sheets instead of full screens.** Choosing a login and saving a new one now slide up over the app you're signing in to.
- **Sync now (Android).** A sync button on the vault screen, which turns while syncing, and **Sync now** in Settings.

## What to try

1. In Chrome, open a site you've saved a login for and tap the username field: your login should be listed; tap it.
2. Lock BlackBoxus, tap the field again, choose **Unlock BlackBoxus to fill**, unlock, and check you're brought back and it fills.
3. Sign in somewhere new, choose **Save**, and check the login appears.

## Found a problem?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ktnreddy/BlackBoxus/issues/new/choose), and never paste a password, your Secret Key or anything from your Emergency Kit. If it could put someone's passwords at risk, use **Report a security problem privately** instead.
