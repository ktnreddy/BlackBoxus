Privacy

BlackBoxus is built to know as little about you as it can. Here is everything it does keep, and why.

What we store

  • Your email address, to find your account when you sign in.
  • Your encrypted vaults and the encrypted copies of your account keys. We can't decrypt any of it.
  • Your public keys, in a public log, so the people you share with can check them.
  • Sign-in sessions, as a hash of each session token, deleted when they expire (after 14 days without use, or 90 days at most).
  • Request counters that slow down password guessing, keyed by a hash of an email address, IP address or session, and deleted daily once their window has passed.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies, in the apps or on this site.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • The desktop app talks only to the BlackBoxus server. The browser extension talks only to the desktop app on your computer.

Who runs the servers

The BlackBoxus server and this site run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare sees the IP address and timing of each request, keeps short-lived request logs to operate the service, and handles them under its own privacy policy. It sees only what our server sees: encrypted data.

Deleting your data

Signing out of a computer removes everything BlackBoxus stored on it. To have your account and its encrypted vaults deleted from the server, contact us through the GitHub repository. A delete option in the app is planned.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be published here and in the source code's history. Last updated November 2026.
