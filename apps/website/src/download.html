Get BlackBoxus

Start with the desktop app. It holds your vaults; the browser extension and Windows Hello work through it.

⚠ This is an early test release. BlackBoxus hasn't been independently audited yet. Try it with test accounts and passwords you can afford to lose, and keep your old password manager for now, and tell us what goes wrong.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Installs for your user account only, with no administrator rights needed.

Download for Windows

Not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it's unrecognised: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Browser extension

Chrome, Edge and Brave, and Firefox 128 or later. Turn on "Browser extension" in the app's Settings, then pair the browser by matching a six-digit code.

For Chrome, Edge, Brave For Firefox

Until it's in the stores, unzip it and load it yourself: in Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. In Firefox, open about:debugging and load its manifest.json (until Firefox restarts). The extension holds no passwords. It asks the unlocked app for one login at a time, only for the site you're on.

Android

Android 9 or later. The same app as on your computer, with screenshots blocked and nothing sent to phone backups.

Download the APK

Not in Google Play yet. Open the file on your phone and allow your browser to install apps when asked. Phones from the last several years (64-bit ARM) are supported.

Linux

64-bit Linux with a desktop. The .deb is for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint; the AppImage runs on most others.

Download the .deb Download the AppImage

Mac, iPhone and iPad apps are being tested and come later.

Your first five minutes

Setting up takes a few steps. The one that matters most is saving your Emergency Kit.

  1. Create your account with your email and a long master password. Make it something you'll remember: nobody, including us, can reset it for you.
  2. Save the Emergency Kit. It holds your Secret Key, which you need with your master password on every new device. Print it or keep the PDF somewhere safe.
  3. Make a recovery key in Settings if you want a way back after forgetting your master password.
  4. Import your passwords from your old password manager's CSV export, then delete that file.
  5. Turn on Windows Hello and the browser extension in Settings.

Check your download

Every release lists the SHA-256 checksum of each file. Compare it with yours before installing.

On Windows, in PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\BlackBoxus-windows-x64-setup.exe. On Linux or a Mac: sha256sum followed by the file's name. The result must match the line for that file in SHA256SUMS.txt.

Each file also has a signed record of the exact commit and build that made it. With the GitHub command-line tool: gh attestation verify FILE -R ktnreddy/BlackBoxus.
